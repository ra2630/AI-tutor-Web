Frequently asked questions

Who is Mentora AI for?

Mentora AI is designed for students who want clear explanations, guided practice, and a learning experience adapted to their goals.

Does it simply give students answers?

No. The product is designed around hints, reasoning, and step-by-step guidance so students remain active participants in learning.

Which subjects will be supported?

The initial vision includes mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, computer science, English, and history, with more subjects added over time.

Is this website connected to a real AI tutor?

This version is a polished static landing page. It can later be connected to authentication, a student dashboard, an AI backend, and a database.
