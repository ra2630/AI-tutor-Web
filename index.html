Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered.

Who is Mentor AI Study for?

The first release is for students preparing for JEE Main and Advanced, including students who need patient foundational teaching before advanced work.

Does it simply give students answers?

No. After a wrong response, the app asks for handwritten working, diagnoses the student's approach, and gives the smallest useful hint. The student must still complete the reasoning and submit the answer.

What is available first?

JEE Mathematics is the first focus, spanning foundations, algebra, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, calculus, vectors, and 3D geometry. JEE Physics and Chemistry, followed by CBSE Class XI and XII, are in development.

Why does the app check a physical notebook?

Writing key ideas and worked solutions creates a durable revision record and keeps the student actively involved. Camera checkpoints verify that required material is present before practice continues.

Can parents see more than a score?

Yes. The Learning Record is designed to show notes, attempts, misconceptions, tutor feedback, and corrected work so parents can understand how learning is improving.
