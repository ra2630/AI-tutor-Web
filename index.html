Pedagogical Engine

Designed for real comprehension, not superficial speedruns

Exam mastery demands disciplined derivation, handwritten verification, and precise diagnostic feedback when errors happen.

01

Patient, First-Principles Classroom Modules

Instead of superficial 2-minute summary cards, modules are taught with the deliberate pacing of an expert classroom teacher. Concepts begin from prerequisites, construct intuitive proofs on the board, address subtle edge cases, and advance to complex examination problems.

Prerequisites → Proof & Intuition → Traps & Edge Cases → Advanced Synthesis
02

Exact Blackboard Records & Physical Notebooks

Passive screen watching fails in competitive exams. As theorems, formulas, diagrams, and teacher-worked solutions appear on the digital board, students maintain an exact, structured physical notebook to build genuine motor memory and a lifetime revision asset.

Notebook Record · Standard Tangents
y = mx ± √(a²m² + b²)  [Point of contact: (-a²m/c, b²/c)]
Rule: Verify m ≠ ∞ before applying slope form.
03

Camera-Verified Notebook Image Checkpoints

To ensure the student is actively writing rather than clicking ahead, Mentor AI includes integrated camera checkpoints. Students photograph their physical notebook notes and rough work for automatic validation before advancing to subsequent modules.

04

Contextual Socratic Doubt Solving (Zero Spoilers)

When a student submits an incorrect response, Mentor AI never dumps the final solution or answers. It analyzes the student's specific step error, isolates the conceptual gap, and asks targeted counter-questions so the student completes the derivation independently.

Student: “Why did my integration by parts fail here?”
Diagnostic: Differentiated e^(2x) correctly but omitted chain factor (1/2) during antiderivative choice.
Tutor Prompt: “What is ∫ e^(2x) dx before applying the uv formula? Correct that term and re-evaluate.”
05

Full-Working CBSE Step Marking

Board exams require rigorous mathematical communication. Mentor AI evaluates student solution steps against standard CBSE marking schemes, awarding explicit fractional marks for formula specification, parameter substitution, intermediate algebra, and final units.

1. Formula stated & boundary values defined +1.0 Mark
2. Algebraic substitution & integration limits +1.5 Marks
3. Final evaluated magnitude & dimensional unit +0.5 Mark
06

Adaptive Weak-Area Practice & Spiral Revision

Instead of random question banks, practice sets adapt in real time to historical misconception patterns. If a student repeatedly falters on sign conventions or trigonometric transformations, the system systematically serves targeted variations until mastery is proven.

Misconception: Sign in Definite Integral Substitution Targeted

Transparent Parent Progress Visibility

Parents receive a clear, verifiable timeline of their student's actual work rather than synthetic scores or vague progress percentages. View timestamped notebook check photos, diagnostic dialogue transcripts, verified study hours, and topic-by-topic concept mastery.

Structured Learning Routine

The 4-stage classroom workflow for every concept

A repeatable, disciplined study rhythm that turns complex concepts into reliable exam problem-solving ability.

Step 01

Blackboard Lecture

Attend the structured concept module. Follow derivations step-by-step with visual geometric or physical intuition.

Step 02

Notebook Record

Transcribe formulas, proof structures, traps, and worked examples into a physical notebook, then submit the photo checkpoint.

Step 03

What curriculum is available in Mentor AI right now? +

JEE Advanced Mathematics is fully available now, comprising 27 modular courses across Algebra, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors, 3D Geometry, and Trigonometry.

JEE Physics, JEE Chemistry, and CBSE Classes XI & XII (Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics) are currently in active development. Additional middle-school classes, NEET, SAT, GRE, and GMAT are on our development roadmap.

How does Mentor AI prevent students from just getting the answer? +

Mentor AI is explicitly built with a zero-spoiler pedagogical architecture. When a student makes an error or asks for help, the AI tutor isolates the exact algebraic or conceptual misstep and offers the smallest effective diagnostic hint or counter-question.

The student must carry out the subsequent mathematical reasoning, compute the steps, and complete the working in their own notebook.

Why does Mentor AI require physical notebook checks? +

Competitive and board examinations are written on physical paper under strict time limits. Passive screen watching creates an illusion of competence without developing essential motor memory or derivation speed.

Our camera checkpoints verify that key definitions, diagrams, traps, and step solutions are physically transcribed in the student's notebook before subsequent practice unlocks.

How does the full-working CBSE step marking work? +

For subjective and board-style questions, Mentor AI evaluates student working against official CBSE marking schemes. Marks are allocated across formula definition (+1M), step substitution (+1.5M), simplification, and final units with concluding statements (+0.5M).

Students learn exactly where marks are lost in subjective examinations even when their numerical intuition is correct.

What information do parents see on the learning record? +

The Parent Learning Record provides verifiable physical evidence: photos of submitted notebook pages, timestamped study and practice sessions, specific misconceptions encountered and resolved, and topic-by-topic concept retention tracking.

Which devices and operating systems are supported? +

Mentor AI is built for mobile devices across iOS and Android. Early access builds and Google Play rollouts are managed through our direct access program. Contact info@mentoraistudy.com for access instructions for your device.
