Privacy Policy for CardBaazi

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This policy explains how CardBaazi handles information when you use the app. We do not sell personal information.

Information handled by CardBaazi

  • Account information: Sign in with Apple or Google provides an account identifier, display name, and email address. These are used for authentication, your player profile, account-scoped progress, safety settings, and credits.
  • Gameplay and purchase information: We process Academy progress, match activity, credit balances, entitlements, and App Store transaction identifiers to provide gameplay and prevent duplicate grants. Apple processes payment details; CardBaazi does not receive your full card or bank information.
  • Table communications: Text chat, reactions, and push-to-talk audio travel between members of an active Epic Online Services lobby. Voice is streamed live and is not intentionally recorded by CardBaazi. If a player submits a safety report, the report reason, optional details, player identifiers, and limited recent chat context may be sent to Epic Online Services.
  • Advertising information: The app includes Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform components for optional rewarded advertising. Rewarded videos are not currently enabled in the App Store build. If enabled in a future version, Google may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and approximate location subject to your region and consent choices.
  • Device storage: The app uses on-device storage for settings, progress, safety preferences, and credit state.

Services we use

  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, StoreKit in-app purchases, and App Store distribution.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, AdMob rewarded-ad components, and User Messaging Platform consent controls.
  • Epic Games: Epic Online Services for device authentication, worldwide lobbies, peer-to-peer gameplay transport, and player reports.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies. Network routing may expose normal connection information, such as an IP address, to service providers and peers as required to establish connectivity.

Your choices and controls

At online tables you can mute a player’s voice, block their communications, or report them. Blocking communication does not interrupt gameplay. If rewarded advertising is enabled in a future version, rewarded videos will remain optional and regional consent controls will be provided where required.

Retention and deletion

Local profile and gameplay information remains until you delete account data in the app or remove the app. Safety reports delivered to Epic and transaction records handled by Apple, Google, or Epic may be retained under those providers’ policies and legal obligations. Contact us for help with a deletion request.

Children

CardBaazi is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security and international use

We use platform authentication, verified StoreKit transactions, consent controls, and authenticated lobby membership to protect app activity. Services may process information in countries other than your own.
