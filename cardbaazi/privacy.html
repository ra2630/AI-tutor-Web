Privacy Policy for CardBaazi

Last updated: August 16, 2026

CardBaazi ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

CardBaazi is designed to be privacy-friendly. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your basic display name and email solely to display your profile in multiplayer rooms and save game stats locally.
  • Microphone Audio: The microphone is used strictly during active Push-to-Talk voice chat in peer-to-peer multiplayer tables. Voice data is streamed directly to peers and is never recorded or stored on any server.
  • Gameplay Data: Match scores and academy progress are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs.

3. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@mentoraistudy.com.
