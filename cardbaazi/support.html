CardBaazi Support & Customer Help

Help for gameplay, worldwide tables, accounts, credits, purchases, and player safety.

Games and Academy

CardBaazi includes Dehla Pakad, Spades, Call Break, Hearts, 3-2-5, Indian Rummy, Klondike, Spider, and FreeCell. Bot matches and Solitaire are free. Seven Academy journeys provide a rules primer and 50 strategy stages per track; stages 1–10 are free.

Signing in

CardBaazi uses Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google. It does not use a separate CardBaazi username or password. If Apple does not share a name after a previous authorization, the app asks you to choose the player name displayed at the table.

Worldwide and private tables

Choose a game, then select Play with Bots, Find a Table, or Private Table. Private-table creators receive a room code to share. Epic Online Services connects players worldwide; no local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth mode is used.

Online credits are charged only when the game begins: 5 credits for the table creator and 1 credit for a joining player. Waiting in or leaving a lobby before the deal is free.

Teams and bots

When a team-game roster is full, each human chooses their own team. The table creator can move bots between teams or remove a bot completely. A connected human cannot be removed by the host.

Credits and purchases

Every newly signed-in account receives 100 starting credits. Later Academy stages unlock in blocks of five.

Credit packs are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Credits do not expire, cannot be transferred, withdrawn, exchanged, or redeemed, and have no cash value. CardBaazi does not offer real-money gambling or cash prizes. Open Profile → Account & Credits to view available packs.

Rewarded videos

Rewarded videos are not currently available in the App Store build. If they are enabled in a future version, they will be optional and shown only when additional credits are needed.

Chat, voice, blocking, and reports

Open table chat and tap the shield to mute voice, block communication, or report a player. Blocking hides chat, reactions, and voice without affecting cards or scoring. Harassment, hateful content, threats, spam, cheating, and offensive player names are not allowed.

Account deletion

Open your profile and select Delete Account & Reset Data to remove local profile, progress, statistics, credit state, and safety preferences. For assistance with service-side information, email support.
