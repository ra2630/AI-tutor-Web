Data Policy

Privacy Principles & Student Learning Records

Last Updated: August 2026

Mentor AI is built for students, parents, and educators preparing for demanding examinations. This document outlines how we collect, store, and utilize learning data strictly for academic feedback, diagnostic progress evaluation, and platform reliability.

1. Learning Evidence & Notebook Submissions

To ensure genuine comprehension and physical study discipline, the platform processes student work records:

  • Notebook Check Photographs: Images of handwritten lecture notes and step-by-step rough work submitted to verify completion of blackboard tasks.
  • Diagnostic Interactions: Student error submissions, intermediate calculation steps, and Socratic guidance dialogue logs.
  • Course Progression: Module completions, timestamped session durations, and adaptive practice topic mastery indicators.

This data is used solely to generate targeted pedagogical feedback, power adaptive revision queues, and populate the verifiable Parent Learning Record.

2. Parent Progress Visibility

Mentor AI provides parents with transparent visibility into genuine study activity. Parents can review verified notebook check submissions, diagnostic interaction summaries, and concept mastery curves to monitor learning progress without relying on opaque synthetic scores.

3. In-App Diagnostics & Problem Reporting

When a student or parent utilizes the in-app Report a problem tool, the application prepares an addressed support draft containing diagnostic metadata:

  • Course identifier and lecture title
  • Specific question or checkpoint identifier
  • Application build number and client OS version (iOS / Android)

This diagnostic context helps our team resolve curriculum or software issues. The system does not automatically attach personal photos, passwords, or unrelated device data.

4. Content Protection & Display Security

To protect proprietary pedagogical materials, curriculum derivations, and examination question items, Mentor AI employs display protections designed to prevent unauthorized automated extraction, screen capture, and third-party recording.

5. No Advertising & No Data Brokering

We do not sell student data, track students across external applications, or display third-party advertisements. Academic data is treated strictly as confidential educational records.

6. Contact & Inquiries

For any questions regarding student data, learning records, or account deletion requests, please contact our support team at support@mentoraistudy.com.
