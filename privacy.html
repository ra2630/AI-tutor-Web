Privacy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Mentor AI Study is being developed for students and families. This page describes the current product principles and will be expanded before public release with the complete legal policy for every enabled service.

Learning evidence

The app may store course progress, question attempts, tutor conversations, and notebook or solution images needed to provide learning feedback and a parent-visible learning record.

Support reports

The in-app reporting tool includes technical context such as app version and stable course identifiers. It does not attach notebook photos, personal details, or answer keys automatically. The sender can review the email before sending.

Content protection

The app uses a secure display layer intended to obscure course material in screenshots, screen recordings, mirroring, and app-switcher previews.

Contact

Questions can be sent to support@mentoraistudy.com.
